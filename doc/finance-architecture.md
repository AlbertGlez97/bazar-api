## Prisma entities (finance)

The overview and the simulator have no tables: they are computed on read.

## Multitenancy

- Every finance row carries `contextId`. The models are registered in `TENANT_SCOPED_MODELS` (`src/database/tenant.extension.ts`), which sets `app.context_id` per transaction.
- PostgreSQL enforces isolation with `ENABLE` and `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` and a `tenant_isolation` policy on `contextId = current_setting('app.context_id', true)`. The application connects with a runtime role without `SUPERUSER` or `BYPASSRLS` (`runtime-database-role.md`).
- Identity: `Account` (shared business login), `Member` (socio or colaborador) and `Device` (authorized with `x-device-token`). `SocioGuard` requires an active socio and an authorized device and rechecks both under `FOR SHARE`; `ContextGuard` accepts any active selected member and authorized device, whatever the role.
- Client-supplied member and device ids must match the validated headers. Context and account are always derived on the server.

## Offline versus online

The backend is request/response and keeps no offline state. The browser decides:

| Module | Client behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Aportaciones, Gastos, Jornadas | Durable local queue in IndexedDB with idempotent replay by client UUIDv7 |
| Resumen, Reservas | Online only |
| Simulador | Ephemeral and online only |
| Acuerdos | Persisted in the backend, online only |
| Pagos de acuerdos | Persisted in the backend, online only |

## Simulator

Pure and read-only: it persists nothing. Money is integer minor units and the engine computes in `BigInt`. The partner pool is `floor(requested x partnerBps / 10000)` and the work pool is the remainder. Partners split EQUAL, with the remainder going to the lowest `memberId`. Work is split by `WORK_SHIFTS` or `KNOWN_MINUTES` with Hamilton largest remainder, so results are exact to the minor unit. Every response carries `SIMULATION_ONLY` and `NOT_A_PAYMENT_OR_OBLIGATION` warnings and a `simulationFingerprint` (`sim1:` plus a sha256 of the canonical JSON).

## Agreements

- A created agreement stores a snapshot: the server re-runs the simulation and rejects the request with 409 `FINANCE_AGREEMENT_SIMULATION_CHANGED` when the client `simulationFingerprint` no longer matches.
- Folio `AC-YYYY-NNNNN`, allocated from a per-context, per-year counter.
- `snapshotFingerprint` is the integrity code of the stored document. Participants (name, role, active flag, work and amounts) are frozen at creation.
- Reversal: one per agreement, with a required reason. A replacement links a new agreement to a voided one through `replacesAgreementId`. There is no `REPLACED` status, only `active` and `voided`.
- Immutability: database triggers reject any UPDATE or DELETE on agreement, participants and reversal.
- Create replay returns the original folio and snapshot with the live status; the reversal `occurredAt` is validated against the agreement date and the server clock (see the contract for details).
- The backend does NOT store the PDF. The frontend generates it on demand from the snapshot.

## Payments (Phase 6)

- A payment records money that was REALLY paid to one participant of an agreement; the system never moves money. Paid = payments without a reversal, pending = agreed - paid, both computed on read; the status (`UNPAID`, `PARTIALLY_PAID`, `PAID`, `NOT_DUE`) is derived, never stored.
- Immutable (triggers on both tables). The only correction is a reversal, then a new payment.
- Overpayment is rejected (`422 FINANCE_PAYMENT_EXCEEDS_PENDING`) under a participant row lock (`READ COMMITTED` plus `FOR SHARE` on the agreement and `FOR UPDATE` on the participant); proven by concurrency tests.
- No impact on the overview, expenses, reserves, sales or the agreement snapshot; `potentiallyAvailableMinor` is not adjusted.
- Contract, errors and concurrency model: `finance-distribution-payments.md`.

## What does not exist

- Withdrawals or transfers: payments exist only as records of money paid outside the system; nothing is executed or moved by La Marchanta.
- Payment methods, receipts or PDFs of a payment, offline payments, atomic replacement of a payment.
- Executed distributions: an agreement records what the participants accepted, and a payment records what was actually paid; neither moves money.
- Reconciliation with cash or bank balances (the overview is a recorded operating result, not a cash balance; payments do not adjust it).
- Digital signature, or backend storage of signed or scanned documents.
- Generated PDFs on the backend.
